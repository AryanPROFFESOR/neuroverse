The Predictive Brain

Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle proposes that the brain is fundamentally a prediction machine. Every perception is a top-down prediction; every sensation is an error signal used to refine those predictions.

💡
Key Insight
The brain never directly perceives reality. It perceives its own predictions, corrected by sensory error signals. Hallucinations are predictions without correction.
